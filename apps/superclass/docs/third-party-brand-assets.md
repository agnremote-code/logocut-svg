# Third-party teaching platform references

Superclass uses text-only compatibility badges for every third-party teaching platform. No third-party logo files are downloaded, modified, bundled, or served.

Reviewed 29 July 2026:

| Platform | Official source reviewed | Decision and usage basis |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Preply | [Preply Terms of Service](https://termsofuse.preply.com/terms_of_use/en_TermsOfService.pdf) | The terms state that Preply marks and logos may not be copied or used without prior written consent. Text-only nominative reference; no logo. |
| italki | Official public site and terms search | No clear public press kit or permission for unrelated commercial product use was located. Text-only nominative reference; no logo. |
| Cambly | [Cambly User Agreement](https://studentsupport.cambly.com/hc/en-us/articles/360038699232-User-Agreement) and tutor agreement | The agreements restrict trademark/logo use without express consent or compliance with brand guidelines. The available ambassador materials apply to authorized ambassadors, which Superclass is not. Text-only nominative reference; no logo. |
| AmazingTalker | Official public site and terms search | No clear public brand-use policy authorizing unrelated commercial compatibility marketing was located. Text-only nominative reference; no logo. |
| Verbling | [Verbling Terms](https://www.verbling.com/terms) | The terms state that the Verbling logo may not be copied or used without prior written permission. Text-only nominative reference; no logo. |

The interface uses careful compatibility language: “designed for teachers who use,” “fits the workflow of tutors teaching through,” and “useful whether you teach privately or through.” It does not claim integration, partnership, approval, sponsorship, or endorsement.

Displayed disclaimer:

> Superclass is an independent product and is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by the teaching platforms mentioned.
